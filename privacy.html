Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GudLabs handles information in connection with Schedule Pilot.

1. Local-first product data

Schedule Pilot is designed as a local-first Windows desktop workspace. Local publishing jobs, schedules, media paths, approval records, creator context and status history may be stored on the creator's computer.

2. TikTok account authorization

Creators connect their own TikTok accounts through TikTok authorization. Schedule Pilot does not require creators to provide their TikTok password to GudLabs. Access is limited to permissions the creator grants.

3. Authorized TikTok data

Depending on the granted scopes, Schedule Pilot may process basic account identity, additional profile information, account statistics and metadata for the creator's public videos. This information is used for connected-account identity, creator insights, analytics, content planning and creator-approved publishing workflows.

4. Content and publishing metadata

The product may process local video metadata, captions, cover selections, privacy and interaction choices, commercial disclosure settings, schedules, approval records and publishing status to prepare and execute creator-approved jobs.

5. AI-assisted metadata and planning

If AI-assisted features are enabled, the information sent to the configured AI provider depends on that provider configuration. AI-generated drafts remain editable and do not themselves approve, schedule, arm or publish a post.

6. Third-party platforms

When a creator requests an authorized TikTok operation, the data needed for that operation is transmitted to TikTok according to TikTok's API, authorization and platform requirements. TikTok has its own privacy practices.

7. Credentials and security

Schedule Pilot is designed not to expose access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets or temporary signed upload URLs through ordinary application responses.

8. Retention and revocation

Local application data can be removed from the creator's device. TikTok authorization can be revoked using applicable TikTok account or developer authorization controls.

9. Contact

Privacy requests: support@gudlabs.xyz.
