TikTok publishing workflow

Preview. Configure. Approve. Schedule. Publish.

Schedule Pilot uses TikTok's authorized publishing capabilities while keeping the creator aware of the account, media and exact post package.

Direct Post — video.publish

Creator-approved Direct Post sends content to the authorized TikTok profile after current Creator Info, post settings, explicit consent and due-time revalidation.

Draft handoff — video.upload

When a creator prefers to finish inside TikTok, Schedule Pilot can send content as a draft for further editing and posting in TikTok.

1. ConnectThe creator authorizes their TikTok account through the supported OAuth flow.
2. PreflightValidate local media, show the target creator and fetch current Creator Info.
3. PreviewShow the actual video and selected cover before approval.
4. ConfigureKeep caption editable and require creator-selected privacy, interactions and disclosure settings.
5. ApproveProtect the exact post package with creator approval.
6. SchedulePersist date, time and timezone. Rescheduling requires fresh arming.
7. Consent & ArmPresent final creator consent before unattended due-time execution.
8. Revalidate & TrackRe-check authorization, Creator Info, media integrity and approved metadata, then track TikTok processing status.

No hidden publishing and no promotional watermark.

Schedule Pilot branding stays in the product interface. Media is not silently modified to promote Schedule Pilot, and automated execution occurs only after creator approval, scheduling and explicit arming.
