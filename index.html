Desktop content operations for authorized TikTok accounts

Organize authorized TikTok accounts from one owner-controlled workspace.

AccountPilot helps account owners and authorized managers connect TikTok accounts, maintain accurate account identity and analytics, organize account strategies, and use AI-assisted planning workflows with explicit owner review.

AccountPilot only works with accounts that the user owns, manages, or is authorized to access. TikTok authorization is handled through official platform authorization mechanisms.

Current capabilities

Authorized Account Identity

Maintain account identifiers, usernames, public profile information, authorization status, and account mappings for connected TikTok accounts.

Account Analytics

Review authorized account statistics and public content metadata made available through supported TikTok APIs.

Strategy & Organization

Organize account positioning, audience, content pillars, workflow rules, and owner-approved operating boundaries.

AI-Assisted Planning

Use configured AI tools to prepare research, structured recommendations, content directions, and editing plans for owner review.

Evidence-Grounded Research

For current-information workflows, AccountPilot can collect public source references and require evidence before AI recommendations proceed.

Owner-Controlled Workflows

Account strategy, AI execution, and other sensitive actions are gated by explicit owner review and authorization.

Local-first operation

AccountPilot is designed primarily as a desktop/local application. Operational account data, workflow records, and application state are kept on the user's own device unless a configured third-party service is explicitly used.

AccountPilot is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
