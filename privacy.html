Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GudLabs handles information in connection with Schedule Pilot.

1. Local-first product data

Schedule Pilot is designed as a local-first desktop workspace. Local publishing jobs, schedules, media paths, approval records and status history may be stored on the creator's computer.

2. Connected platform authorization

When a creator connects a supported platform account, authorization credentials and account identifiers are used only to perform creator-authorized platform operations. Schedule Pilot does not require creators to disclose their TikTok password to GudLabs.

3. Content and metadata

The product may process local video metadata, captions, cover selections, privacy choices, interaction settings, commercial disclosure settings, schedules and publishing status for the purpose of preparing and executing creator-approved publishing jobs.

4. AI-assisted metadata

If AI-assisted caption preparation is enabled, the information sent to the configured AI provider depends on that provider configuration. Creator drafts remain editable and AI generation does not itself approve, schedule or publish a post.

5. Third-party platforms

When a creator chooses to publish, necessary media and metadata are transmitted to the connected platform according to its authorization and API requirements. Third-party services have their own privacy policies.

6. Security

Schedule Pilot is designed not to expose access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets or temporary signed upload URLs through ordinary application responses.

7. Retention and deletion

Creators can remove local application data by uninstalling the product and deleting its local data directory. Connected-platform authorization can be revoked through the relevant platform controls.

8. Contact

Privacy requests: support@gudlabs.xyz.
