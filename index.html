What it solves

A publishing workflow built around review, not blind automation.

Creators keep control of the account, media, caption, privacy, interaction settings, commercial disclosure and final approval. Automation begins only after the creator has reviewed and authorized the post package.

01

Connected accounts

Authorize supported accounts through official platform authorization and keep account identity visible throughout the publishing workflow.

02

Reviewable post packages

Preview media, edit captions, choose privacy and interactions, review disclosure settings, and approve the exact package that will be sent.

03

Scheduled execution

Approved posts can be scheduled. At due time the application revalidates the authorization, media and approved settings before sending the post.

Creator awareness

Nothing is published without an approved package.

Changes to protected post fields invalidate approval and require a fresh creator review. Publishing status is tracked after submission so creators can see whether a post is processing, complete or blocked.

Who it is for

Designed for real creator workflows.

Independent creators

Prepare and schedule original content while retaining final control over what is posted.

Creator teams

Coordinate review and publishing across authorized creator accounts without sharing platform passwords.

Content operations

Use a consistent queue, approvals, status history and account-specific policies for recurring publishing work.
