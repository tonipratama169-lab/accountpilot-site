Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

AccountPilot is a desktop account management and analytics application for authorized social media accounts.

Information processed

  • Platform account identifiers used to keep account mappings accurate.
  • Username and public profile link.
  • Authorized account statistics such as follower, following, like, and video counts.
  • Public video metadata available through authorized platform APIs.
  • Authorization status and synchronization timestamps.

Local-first storage

AccountPilot is designed to keep its operational account registry and application data on the user's own device. The public AccountPilot website does not contain the user's account registry, social media passwords, or private application database.

How information is used

Information is used to provide requested account identity synchronization, analytics, account organization, and content-history features.

Sharing and sale

AccountPilot does not sell personal data. Data is transmitted to a supported platform only as needed to use that platform's authorized APIs or authentication services.

Authorization and revocation

Users control which supported platform accounts they authorize and may revoke access through the relevant platform settings.

Security

AccountPilot is designed to use official authorization mechanisms where supported. Users remain responsible for securing their computer and connected platform accounts.

Changes

This policy may be updated as AccountPilot evolves. The latest version will be published here.
