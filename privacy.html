Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AccountPilot processes information when a user connects and manages authorized TikTok accounts through the AccountPilot desktop application.

1. Information AccountPilot may process

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, AccountPilot may process:

  • TikTok account identifiers used to maintain accurate account mappings;
  • username, display name, profile link, and other authorized or public profile information;
  • authorized account statistics such as follower, following, like, and video counts;
  • public video metadata available through authorized TikTok APIs;
  • authorization scopes, authorization status, synchronization timestamps, and OAuth access or refresh tokens used to maintain authorized connections;
  • account strategies, workflow settings, owner approvals, AI work orders, and related application records;
  • AI-generated recommendations and user-approved or user-rejected workflow results;
  • public research-source references and evidence metadata used for current-information workflows; and
  • content-planning or publishing-planning metadata entered by the user when those features are used.

2. Information AccountPilot does not request

AccountPilot does not require users to provide their TikTok password directly to AccountPilot. TikTok sign-in and consent are handled through official TikTok authorization mechanisms.

3. Local-first storage

AccountPilot is designed primarily as a local desktop application. Its operational account registry, workflow records, authorization metadata, and application state are generally stored on the user's own device. The public AccountPilot website does not contain the user's private AccountPilot database or TikTok password.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • connect and verify authorized TikTok accounts;
  • maintain account identity mappings and authorization state;
  • display account analytics and authorized public content information;
  • organize account strategies, content workflows, and owner-review processes;
  • prepare AI-assisted research, recommendations, content directions, and editing plans;
  • support content-planning or publishing-related workflows when enabled and authorized; and
  • maintain audit history, safety checks, and application integrity.

5. AI processing

AccountPilot may use a user-configured AI provider for AI-assisted features. If a local AI provider is used, relevant processing may remain on the user's device. If the user explicitly configures and permits a remote AI provider, information included in an AI request may be transmitted to that provider and will also be subject to that provider's terms and privacy practices.

6. Public research sources

For workflows that require current information, AccountPilot may retrieve and store references to public web sources, including source URLs, publisher names, titles, publication timestamps, and evidence identifiers. These references are used to ground AI-assisted recommendations and safety review.

7. Sharing and sale

AccountPilot does not sell personal data. Information may be transmitted to TikTok as necessary to use TikTok's authorized APIs and authentication services, or to another provider explicitly configured by the user for a requested AccountPilot feature.

8. Authorization, revocation, and deletion

Users control which TikTok accounts they authorize. TikTok authorization may be revoked through the relevant TikTok account settings. Because AccountPilot is local-first, users control retention of the local AccountPilot installation and its local data on their device. Users who need assistance with AccountPilot data removal may contact boslmstore@gmail.com.

9. Retention

Local AccountPilot records may remain on the user's device until the user removes them, deletes the local application data, or otherwise ends the relevant workflow. Authorization tokens may also become invalid when they expire or when platform access is revoked.

10. Security

AccountPilot is designed to use official authorization mechanisms where supported and to keep operational data local by default. Users remain responsible for securing their device, connected TikTok accounts, configured providers, and local AccountPilot installation.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or data-related requests may be sent to boslmstore@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as AccountPilot evolves. The latest version will be published at this URL.

TikTok is a third-party service. AccountPilot is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
