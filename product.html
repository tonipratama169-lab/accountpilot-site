Product

One creator workspace. Clear capability ownership.

Schedule Pilot combines creator-facing account context, planning and publishing without collapsing every internal service into one monolithic process.

Connected Accounts

Identify the authorized creator using TikTok account identity and permission state. Credentials are not exposed through ordinary product screens.

Creator Insights

Use authorized profile fields, engagement statistics and recent public videos to provide account-specific context for creator decisions.

Content Planning

Turn creator context into recommendations and content direction. Planning remains separate from final publishing authority.

Publishing Queue

Preview media, configure metadata, approve packages, schedule, arm and track creator-approved posts.

Publishing Metadata AI

Generate editable caption and metadata drafts. AI output cannot silently approve, schedule, arm or publish a post.

Draft & Direct Post

Support TikTok draft handoff through video.upload and creator-approved Direct Post through video.publish when the account and app are eligible.
