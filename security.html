Security

Creator authorization. Capability boundaries. Fail-closed publishing.

Schedule Pilot keeps analytics, planning and publishing responsibilities separated internally while presenting creators with one consistent product workflow.

Least-privilege authorization

Connected TikTok data and actions depend on the scopes granted by the creator. Authorization does not imply permission beyond those scopes.

Protected credentials

Ordinary UI and API responses are designed not to expose access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets or temporary signed upload URLs.

Publishing integrity

Creator approval, schedule state and due-time validation are separated. Changed protected settings invalidate stale approval or arming where required.

Duplicate prevention

Ambiguous Direct Post initialization is handled fail-closed rather than automatically repeated when a duplicate post could result.
