Creator access
TikTok publishing integration

Creator-reviewed publishing, followed by scheduled execution.

The TikTok publishing workflow is designed around the Content Posting API requirements: current creator information, editable metadata, explicit consent, protected approval snapshots and visible post status.

End-to-end flow

1. Connect
Creator authorizes the account.
2. Preview
Review the exact media.
3. Configure
Choose title, privacy, interactions and disclosure.
4. Consent
Creator expressly approves the post package.
5. Schedule
Set a due time for the approved package.
6. Track
Follow processing through final status.

Latest creator information

Before the posting screen is finalized, AccountPilot retrieves the latest creator information and uses platform-provided capabilities for privacy and interaction controls.

No hidden defaults

Privacy is chosen by the creator. Comment, Duet and Stitch are enabled only when available and expressly selected by the creator.

Commercial disclosure

Creators can indicate promotional or branded content and receive the corresponding disclosure and consent language before publishing.

Immutable approval

If protected media or post settings change after approval, AccountPilot invalidates the approval and requires another review before sending content.

Scheduled execution

At due time the app revalidates the target account, authorization, media and approved package before starting the post request.

Status visibility

After submission, AccountPilot tracks platform processing status and surfaces completion or failure states to the creator.

Platform availability: Publishing capabilities, privacy options, limits and public visibility depend on TikTok authorization, creator eligibility, API-client audit status and TikTok platform policies.
