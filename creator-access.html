Access

Managed onboarding for creators and content teams.

AccountPilot is currently distributed through managed onboarding. Creators and teams interested in using the product can request access directly from GudLabs.

Request AccountPilot accessReview the product
What to include: your name or team name, intended creator publishing use case, approximate number of accounts you are authorized to manage, and a contact email. GudLabs may request additional information needed for onboarding and support.

What managed onboarding means

Access is not presented as an open self-service download. GudLabs coordinates product access, setup and support directly with approved users during the current distribution phase.

Authorized use only

Users may connect and operate only TikTok accounts they own or are expressly authorized to manage. AccountPilot does not grant rights to third-party accounts or content.

Platform authorization

Connected TikTok operations remain subject to TikTok authorization, API permissions, account eligibility, platform availability and policy requirements.
