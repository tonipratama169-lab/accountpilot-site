Product

One desktop workspace for creator-approved publishing.

Schedule Pilot focuses on the publishing capability: preparation metadata, media preview, cover selection, approval, persistent scheduling, due-time validation and publishing status.

Publishing Queue

Track drafts, approved packages, scheduled jobs, execution blocks and completed posts.

Automatic preflight

Validate local media and retrieve current creator capability information before the creator configures the final post.

Smart Cover

Review representative local frames and choose the cover moment. Recommendations never override creator choice.

Persistent schedule

Date, time and timezone are stored as durable job state. Rescheduling requires re-arming.

Publishing Metadata AI

Generate editable caption drafts using Schedule Pilot's publishing metadata capability. AI output cannot approve, arm or publish a job.

Status & recovery evidence

Track publish identifiers, upload progress, processing state and policy/network failures without unsafe duplicate initialization.
