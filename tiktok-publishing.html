TikTok publishing workflow

Current creator information. Editable metadata. Explicit consent.

For supported TikTok publishing, Schedule Pilot uses authorized platform APIs and keeps the creator aware of the account, media and post package throughout the workflow.

1. ConnectAuthorize the creator account through the supported OAuth flow.
2. PreviewReview the actual local video and account identity.
3. ConfigureChoose privacy, interactions, disclosure, caption and cover.
4. ApproveApprove the exact protected post package.
5. ScheduleChoose a persistent date, time and timezone.
6. Consent & ArmExplicitly confirm the platform declaration before unattended execution.
7. RevalidateAt due time re-check authorization, creator info, media and approved fields.
8. TrackFollow processing status after submission.

No promotional watermark is added to creator media.

Schedule Pilot's product branding stays in the application interface and website, not superimposed on the content being sent to a creator's account.
