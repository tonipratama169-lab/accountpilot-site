One workspace from account connection to publishing history.

AccountPilot combines creator account connection, a focused publishing composer, schedule management and analytics without forcing users to jump between separate products.

Core capabilities

Designed around the publishing job lifecycle.

Account connection

Authorized creator accounts

Connect supported TikTok accounts and refresh account information using the product's official API integration.

Composer

Video and post preparation

Select the final local video, choose the destination creator, prepare an editable caption and select a representative cover frame.

Review

Creator-facing publishing choices

Final availability is refreshed from current creator information. Privacy and supported interaction choices remain explicit.

Scheduling

Persistent date, time and timezone

Publishing jobs can be scheduled and managed through Queue and Calendar views, with approval/arming state preserved as part of the job lifecycle.

Delivery

Official Direct Post transport

Creator-selected local media is submitted through the configured TikTok Content Posting API workflow using FILE_UPLOAD.

Evidence

Publishing history & analytics

Operational status and durable publish identifiers support auditability, while account analytics remain available in the same workspace.

Optional assistance

Caption assistance remains editable.

AccountPilot may offer optional caption drafting assistance. Suggested text remains a draft and does not itself approve, schedule or publish a post.
